Why use Lost & Found Portal?

We make reuniting with your belongings effortless through smart technology and a verified community.

Smart Auto-Matching

Our algorithm automatically compares lost and found posts, instantly notifying you if we detect a high-confidence match based on keywords, category, and location.

Geo-location Tagging

Pinpoint exactly where you lost or found an item on the interactive map. Narrow down search radii to find matches near your specific incident area.

Secure Claim Verification

Protect your items. Claimants must provide secure proof of ownership (like passwords, distinct marks, or receipts) before items are handed over.

Real-time Chat

Communicate securely within the platform. Discuss handover locations and verify details without exposing your personal phone number or email.

Instant Alerts

Set up keyword alerts. Lose your 'Blue MacBook'? Get an instant notification the second someone reports finding an item matching that description.

Verified Community

A trusted ecosystem. We require email verification for all users, keeping the platform secure, transparent, and spam-free for everyone.
